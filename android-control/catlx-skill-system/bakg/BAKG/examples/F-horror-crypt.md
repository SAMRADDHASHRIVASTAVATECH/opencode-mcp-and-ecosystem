# Example F — Horror short: candle-lit crypt, shadow creature that only moves in darkness

Full traversal: `knowledge/system/04-ultimate-test.md` §4.6.

## World rule (D02)
The creature only moves in darkness. The candle is the timer. Lighting ENFORCES the story.

## Key choices
- Acting: stillness is the weapon (D75)
- Camera: low POV, Dutch angles, ECU of candle (Ch 21)
- Creature: silhouette only until the final frame (D04, D104)
- Lighting: practical candle, crushed blacks, volume dust (Ch 22 mood recipes)
- Comp: desaturated + warm candle accents, grain, vignette
- Sound: reverb crypt, candle crackle, silence, sub-drop (Ch 23)

## Lesson
Horror is restraint. Negative space, silhouette, atmosphere, and sound do the work. Animation principles applied: anticipation and stillness, not motion.
