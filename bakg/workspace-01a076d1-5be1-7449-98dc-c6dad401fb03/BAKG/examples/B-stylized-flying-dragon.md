# Example B — A stylized flying dragon

Full traversal: `knowledge/system/04-ultimate-test.md` §4.2.

## Decompose
- Entity: dragon (flying creature, wings, tail)
- Style: stylized (not realistic anatomy)
- Action: flying

## Key choices
- Style: stylized path (5.1) — simplified anatomy, bold PBR or cel
- Modeling: box+subdiv or sculpt-first for organic (5.3)
- Rig: wing hierarchy + IK wingtip + fold + tail chain (5.4, Ch 28.7/28.8)
- Feathers vs membrane: decision 5.5
- Flight: illusion (D72) — weight and air from wing speed, body pitch, glide
- Engine: EEVEE often enough for stylized

## Lesson
Stylized does **not** mean skip anatomy. Caricature is anatomy with exaggeration (D21). Flight is 90% illusion.
